# Chatto: Social Prediction Markets Platform
## Seedify Prediction Markets Hackathon 2025

## 🎯 The Problem

**Current prediction markets are isolated and lack social engagement**

- Prediction platforms feel transactional, not community-driven
- Limited real-time discussion around predictions
- No social proof or collaborative intelligence
- Difficult to discover trending predictions
- Missing the human element in market dynamics

---

## 💡 Our Solution: Chatto

**The first social-first prediction markets platform**

Chatto combines the power of prediction markets with real-time social interaction, creating a new paradigm where:

- **Chat** drives market discovery
- **Community** validates predictions
- **Social proof** enhances market accuracy
- **Real-time discussions** inform better decisions

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. **Social Prediction Feeds**
- Discover trending predictions through your social feed
- See what markets your network is trading
- Comment, react, and discuss predictions in real-time

### 2. **Live Market Chats**
- Every prediction market has a dedicated chat room
- Real-time discussions influence market sentiment
- AI-powered sentiment analysis from chat data

### 3. **Community Intelligence**
- Collaborative prediction pools
- Expert leaderboards and reputation systems
- Social proof signals on market positions

### 4. **Interactive Events & Places**
- Location-based prediction markets
- Event-specific markets with live updates
- Community gathering spaces for major predictions

### **Frontend**

- Apollo Client for GraphQL subscriptions
- Real-time WebSocket connections
- Responsive, mobile-first design

### **Backend**
- Node.js + Express + TypeScript
- GraphQL API with Apollo Server
- MongoDB for scalable data storage
- Real-time subscriptions via GraphQL-WS

### **Blockchain Integration** (In Progress)

- Transparent, verifiable outcomes

### **AI Enhancement**
- OpenAI integration for sentiment analysis
- Market trend predictions from social signals
- Automated market insights
